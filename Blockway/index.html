Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · App: Blockway (com.bolong.blockway)

Blockway is a single-player puzzle game published by an independent developer under the Google Play account android.bolong@gmail.com. This policy explains what information is processed when you play, what stays on your device, and what choices you have.

Short version: the game has no account, no sign-up and no chat. Your progress stays on your device. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, purchases are handled by Google Play, and consent (where required) is collected through Google UMP.

1. Information we process

The game itself does not require registration and does not send your personal data to our servers. The following values are stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, best move counts and completed levels.
  • In-game economy: coin balance, daily-reward streak and the date of your last claim.
  • Settings: sound, vibration and ad-removal status.
  • A local counter used to limit how often ads are shown.

Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data are processed by Google AdMob as described in sections 5 and 6. We do not receive that data ourselves; we only see aggregated revenue reports.

2. How we use it

Because we do not collect personal information on our own systems, there is nothing for us to sell or share. The data stored on your device is used solely to:

  • restore your level progress, coins and daily streak the next time you open the app;
  • apply your sound and vibration preferences;
  • remember that you purchased "Remove Ads" so full-screen ads are not shown;
  • enforce daily limits on rewarded and interstitial advertising.

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. App permissions

Blockway requests only the permissions required by the game, advertising and billing components:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to load ads and contact Google Play for purchases. The game itself is fully playable offline.
  • VIBRATE — optional haptic feedback when you move a block or complete a level. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — required for the optional "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — allows the Google Mobile Ads SDK to access the advertising ID, subject to your consent choices.

The app requests no storage, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.

4. Storage and security

All game data is kept inside the app's private directory on your device. Android's application sandbox prevents other apps from reading it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. We do not operate a server and therefore never receive, store or process your game data. The app disables Android's automatic cloud backup for its preferences. Network requests made by the advertising and billing SDKs use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. Third-party services

The app integrates the following services provided by Google:

  • Google AdMob — serves the rewarded and interstitial ads that keep the game free.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. We never receive or store your payment card details.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — collects and stores your advertising consent choices where required by law.

These services operate under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Apart from them, the app integrates no analytics, crash-reporting, tracking or social-media SDKs.

6. Advertising and your choices

Blockway is free and is funded by advertising. The app shows optional rewarded videos (for example, to earn coins) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To select, deliver and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and share:

  • your advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a coarse region;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, reward completion) and diagnostic data.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland), a Google-certified consent message is shown before ads are requested. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalization in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can change or withdraw your consent choice by clearing the app's data or reinstalling the app.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

7. Your rights and GDPR

  • Data controller. The developer of Blockway is the data controller. Because the game does not collect personal data on our own systems, there is no personal data record for us to administer.
  • Legal basis. The game itself processes no personal data, so no legal basis under Article 6 is required for it. Advertising-related processing is carried out by Google as described above.
  • Your rights. Even though we hold no personal data, you retain all your rights, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection.
  • Advertising data. To exercise these rights with respect to advertising data, use Google's privacy controls at https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • Complaints. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Blockway is a puzzle game intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about the advertising shown in the app, you can disable ad personalization in the system settings. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will act promptly.

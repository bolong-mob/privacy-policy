Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · App: Blockway (com.bolong.blockway)

Blockway is a single-player puzzle game published by an independent developer under the Google Play account android.bolong@gmail.com. This page explains what information is processed when you play.

Short version: the game has no account, no sign-up and no chat. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and purchases are handled by Google Play.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, best move counts, coins and daily-reward streak.
  • Settings: sound, vibration and ad-removal status.

These values are stored in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded to our servers. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Blockway shows optional rewarded videos (to earn coins) and occasional full-screen ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • Device or other identifiers (including the advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • App interactions and ad interactions
  • Diagnostics and performance data

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland), a Google-certified consent message is shown before ads are requested. You can change your choice later in the app's settings or through your device's ad settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" product is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google provides us only with the purchase status needed to unlock the feature.

4. Children

Blockway is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data retention and deletion

We do not operate a server that stores your game data. Local data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. For advertising data, use Google's ad settings and privacy controls. You may also contact us to ask questions about deletion.

6. Security

Game data is kept in the app's sandbox. Network requests made by the advertising and billing SDKs use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email android.bolong@gmail.com.

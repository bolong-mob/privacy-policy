1. Introduction

3D BLOCK (hereinafter "this app", "we", "us") is a 3D block puzzle game for Android. Pieces fall toward a rotating metal tower; you rotate the tower, place each piece and fill complete rings to shatter them. The app offers two game modes, metal skins, optional helper features and an optional one-time premium upgrade.

We believe privacy should be simple. This policy explains how the app handles information, how that information is protected and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not collect, upload or store your personal information on any server operated by us, and it does not require registration or sign-in. Specifically:

  • No account data. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • No device identifiers. We do not collect IMEI, Android ID, MAC address or advertising identifiers.
  • No location data. The app does not request location permission and never reads your location.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. The app contains no analytics or advertising software and builds no user profiles of any kind.

All data the app produces stays on your own device:

  • Game progress, scores, best scores, the selected skin, language, sound/haptic and quality preferences are saved in the app's local storage.
  • Your premium upgrade entitlement is recorded locally and verified through Google Play services.

3. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to use, sell or share with third parties. The data stored on your device is used solely to:

  • restore your game, scores and daily helper allowances the next time you open the app;
  • apply your preferences, such as skin, language, sound, haptics, graphics quality and control style;
  • remember whether you have purchased the premium upgrade.

Information you actively choose to send:

  • Feedback email: if you tap "Feedback", your own email app opens with our support address pre-filled. Whether you send it, and what you write, is entirely your choice. We use the message only to reply to you.
  • Share: the share feature saves a screenshot on your device and hands it to the sharing app you choose. We do not receive or upload the screenshot.

You can erase all local data at any time by clearing the app's data in the system settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. In-App Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time premium upgrade, which unlocks unlimited Freeze-Time and the Auto-Play helper permanently. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card number, billing address or other payment information — this is handled by Google.

Every game mode and core feature remains playable without purchasing, and the helper features can be tried for free. Your purchase is verified through Google Play services and the entitlement is kept on your device; you can restore it on the same Google account using the "Restore" option.

5. App Permissions

The app requests only the following permissions:

  • INTERNET / network access: used for Google Play in-app purchase verification and for opening store, privacy or feedback links. The game itself is fully playable offline.
  • VIBRATE: used for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off at any time in Settings.

The app requests no storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or call-log permissions, and it does not read your photos or files.

6. Data Storage and Security

All game data is kept inside the app's private storage on your device. Android's application sandbox prevents other apps from reading it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

We do not operate any game server and therefore never receive, store or process your game data. Communication with Google Play for purchase verification is protected by Google using encryption in transit. If Android's system backup is enabled on your device, app data may be included in your own Google account backup; you can control this in the system settings, and we have no access to it.

7. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service the app interacts with is Google Play (distribution and in-app purchase billing). Information processed by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The app integrates no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, tracking or social-media SDKs.

8. Your Rights and GDPR

In line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, we confirm the following:

  • Data controller. The developer of 3D BLOCK is the data controller. Because the app does not collect personal data on our own systems, there is no personal data record for us to administer.
  • Legal basis. The app itself processes no personal data, so no legal basis under Article 6 is required for it. Purchase processing is carried out by Google under its own legal bases.
  • Your rights. Even though we hold no personal data, you retain all your legal rights, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. For data held by Google, please use Google's account and privacy controls.
  • Complaints. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

3D BLOCK is a puzzle game suitable for a general audience. It contains no realistic violence and no personal, location or advertising data collection. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because the app collects no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will act promptly.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. A new version will always be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

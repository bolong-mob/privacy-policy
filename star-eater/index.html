1. Introduction

Star Eater (hereinafter "this app") is an offline arcade game for Android. You steer a black hole across eight star regions, devour asteroids, planets, gas giants, stars and rival black holes, and grow large enough to reach the target mass before the match timer runs out.

The game is published in two editions, which differ in how they are funded:

  • Star Eater – the free edition (package name com.starteater.game). It is supported by advertising and shows rewarded video and interstitial ads.
  • Star Eater: Cosmos Edition – the paid edition (package name com.starteater.game.cosmos). It contains no advertising code at all.

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what the app does with your information, how that information is protected and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

The game itself does not collect, upload or store your personal information, and it does not require registration or sign-in. Specifically:

  • No account data. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • No device identifiers collected by us. We do not collect IMEI, Android ID, MAC address or similar identifiers for ourselves.
  • No location data. The app does not request location permission and never reads your location.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own. The app contains no analytics SDK and does not build user profiles.
  • No access to your files, camera, microphone or contacts. The app never asks for these permissions.

The only data the app produces stays on your own device:

  • Game progress: your level, experience, stardust, energy and its regeneration timestamp, region completion and star ratings, unlocked codex entries, owned and equipped skins, and Star Pass progress.
  • Your settings: music and sound effect volume, visual quality and effect density, colour-blind mode, high-contrast mode, text scaling, battery saver, and your chosen interface language.
  • Daily counters and timestamps used to enforce the advertising frequency limits described in section 7.
  • A "ghost" record of your most recent run – the display name currently set in the game, your final mass, the rating you earned and a short series of mass samples over time. This record is used only so that you can race against your own previous run in a later match. It never leaves your device.

3. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to use, sell or share with third parties. The data stored on your device is used solely to:

  • restore your progress, energy and unlocks the next time you open the app;
  • apply your preferences, such as language, audio, visual quality and accessibility settings;
  • enforce the daily limits and cooldowns on advertising so that the game is never overwhelmed by ads;
  • replay your own previous run as a "ghost" rival inside the game.

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's data in the system settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. App Permissions

Star Eater: Cosmos Edition is fully offline and requests no permissions whatsoever.

The free edition requests only the permissions required by Google AdMob to load and measure advertising:

  • INTERNET – to download ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – to check whether a connection is available before requesting an ad, so that the game does not stall when you are offline.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID – the advertising ID permission used by Google AdMob to select, deliver and measure ads.

Neither edition requests storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or phone permissions. The core game is fully playable offline; without a network connection, only the optional reward videos and interstitials are unavailable.

You can review or revoke permissions at any time in Android's system settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

All game data is kept inside the app's private directory on your device. Android's application sandbox prevents other apps from reading it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

We do not operate any server and therefore never receive, store or process your game data. If Android's system backup is enabled on your device, your app data may be included in your own Google account backup; you can control this in the system settings, and we have no access to it.

6. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following services, which are provided by Google and are used only where described:

  • Google AdMob – serves the rewarded video and interstitial ads in the free edition. Cosmos Edition does not include this component.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) – used in the free edition to ask for and store your advertising consent where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.
  • Google Play – distributes the app, processes the purchase of Cosmos Edition and, where available, any in-app purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive or store your payment details.

These services operate under Google's own privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Apart from them, the app integrates no analytics, tracking or social-media SDKs.

7. Advertising and Your Choices

The free edition is funded by advertising. Two formats are used, and both are deliberately restrained:

  • Rewarded video – shown only when you actively choose to watch it, in order to claim an extra reward such as reviving a run, doubling the stardust you earned, or topping up energy. You are never forced to watch one.
  • Interstitial – shown only at a natural pause between matches, at most twice per day and never more often than once every ten minutes.

To select, deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your advertising ID, device model and approximate region. We, the developers, never receive your personal data from AdMob; we only see aggregated revenue reports that contain no information about individual users.

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalization at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Where the law requires it, ad personalization depends on the consent choices collected through Google's consent tools; you can review or change those choices at any time.
  • Removing ads inside the free edition, or using Cosmos Edition, means no ads are requested or shown at all.

8. Your Rights and GDPR

In line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and similar laws, we confirm the following:

  • Data controller. The developer of Star Eater is the data controller. Because the game does not collect personal data on our own systems, there is no personal data record for us to administer.
  • Legal basis. The game itself processes no personal data, so no legal basis under Article 6 is required for it. Advertising-related processing is carried out by Google as described above, and where consent is required it is collected through Google's consent tools before any personalized ads are requested.
  • Your rights. Even though we hold no personal data, you retain all your rights, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection.
  • Advertising data. To exercise these rights with respect to advertising data, please use Google's privacy controls at https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • California and other US states. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. Game data never leaves your device and we hold no personal information about you.
  • Complaints. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

Star Eater is an arcade game suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and because the app collects no personal data at all, there is no children's data for us to hold.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about the advertising shown in the free edition, you can choose the ad-free Cosmos Edition or disable ad personalization in the system settings as described above. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will act promptly.

10. Policy Changes
